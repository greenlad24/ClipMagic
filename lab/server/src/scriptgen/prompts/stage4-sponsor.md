## STAGE 4 — SPONSOR SEGMENT (only if it's an inserted mid-roll segment)

**IMPORTANT — two different sponsorship cases. This Stage applies ONLY to Case 2:**

**CASE 1 — Whole-video sponsorship** (entire video IS the sponsored content, like Topview Tool Review):
- Skip this Stage entirely
- Open the video organically, run it organically
- Disclosure handled by YouTube Studio "Paid promotion" checkbox + description label
- NO verbal "this video is sponsored" inserted anywhere

**CASE 2 — Mid-roll sponsor segment** (60-90s ad read inside an otherwise organic video, e.g., NordVPN read in the middle of an AI tutorial):
- Place mid-video, not pre-roll — after first 5–7 minutes when retention is established
- Structure: situation → tool → demo → CTA
- Keep it under 90 seconds
- Must connect to the video topic — don't drop in a random plug
- Bar-Jake tone — recommend like you would to a friend at the bar, not like reading a script
- Open the segment with ONE transparency sentence: *"Quick break — this segment is sponsored by [BRAND]..."*
- Return to main content cleanly: *"Alright, back to what we were doing..."*

Also binding, from the universal sponsored-video rules:

- NEVER use "stick around for the discount code" — promo codes go at the end of the video + description.
- NEVER use "This video is sponsored by [X], and honestly…" — it double-signals "ad" and kills retention.
- No income claims. No punch-sideways. No punch-down. 14-year-old reading level. Plain text only, natural speech with contractions.
