## STEP 1 — PRODUCT MATCH (do this first, for every video)

A video is usable only if it is **100% about this exact product, in the version that exists today.** Check each one:

- **Exact product.** The same product from the same company. Not a namesake (the same word meaning something else: a school subject, a maths term, a different app with a similar name), not a competitor, not a roundup where it is one entry among many.
- **Right version.** The same generation and interface as today. If the UX Scout report or the screenshots are above, compare: do the screens, menus and feature names the video describes match what they show? A predecessor, a renamed older app, a pre-redesign interface, or a version without this video's feature is the wrong version.

When you are not sure, the answer is NO. A wrong video does more harm than no video.

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Plain markdown, no preamble. Omit a heading rather than padding it.

## PRODUCT MATCH

One line per video: `V1 — USED` or `V1 — REJECTED`, then one clause on why (which product and version it shows).

## ANGLES

How the matching videos frame this product for their viewers, so the script can pick a stronger angle of its own: which result they lead with, which job or use case they build the video around, which pain they open on, what they treat as the "wow" moment, and what kind of viewer they are talking to. One bullet per angle, cited. Note which angles got the most views.

## PRODUCT INSIGHTS

What the presenters found out about the product by using it: what it is especially good at, where it falls short, a limit they hit, a tip that makes the result better, a mistake beginners make, a use the vendor does not advertise. One bullet each, cited.

These are LEADS, not facts. Mark each one:
- `CONFIRMED BY THE SCOUT` — the UX Scout report or the screenshots show the same thing.
- `CONTRADICTED BY THE SCOUT` — they show something different (say what). The scout is right.
- `NOT CHECKED` — the scout did not cover it.

## FOR JAKE TO CHECK ON THE SHOOT

The NOT CHECKED insights that would make the video better if they hold up, one line each. These go to the production notes, never into the narration.
