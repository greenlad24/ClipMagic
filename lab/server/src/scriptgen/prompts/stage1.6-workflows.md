## WHAT YOU ARE TAKING, AND WHAT YOU ARE NOT

**Take the procedure. Never the words.**

You are extracting facts: what the screen is called, what gets clicked, in what order, what gets typed, what setting gets changed, what goes wrong and how it is fixed. Those are facts about a product, and facts are free to anyone who checks them.

You are NOT extracting sentences. Do not copy a phrase, an analogy, a joke, a transition, or a way of explaining something. Not one line. Everything you write here gets rewritten in Jake's voice later anyway, so a borrowed sentence helps nobody and takes something that isn't ours.
