Schedule messages from inside any WhatsApp chat by sending yourself a command. The linked device does the rest.

How to schedule

Type one of these in a chat (case-insensitive triggers: /schedule, /sched, /s):

  • /schedule tomorrow 9am : Standup reminder
  • /schedule in 2h : Call the plumber
  • /schedule monday 9am to +44 7911 123456 : Invoice is due
  • /s 2026-08-01 18:30 : Happy birthday!

Grammar: <trigger> <when…> [to <number>] : <message>. Without to, it sends into the chat you typed it in. Weekend times get a Monday nudge in the reply.
