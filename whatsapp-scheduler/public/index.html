What happens after you link

  1. Scan the code above with a dedicated / secondary number. It links to your lab account only — each signed-in user has a separate WhatsApp session, chats and schedule.
  2. This page turns into a composer: pick one of your existing chats, say when (“Monday morning”), and write the message.
  3. It sends itself at that time — even while your phone is asleep. Nothing reaches the recipient beforehand.

Times are read in Asia/Bangkok. Recipients can only be chats you already have, and no phone numbers are stored on the server.

⚠️ Automating a personal WhatsApp number is against WhatsApp's terms and can get it banned — use a secondary number and keep volume low.
