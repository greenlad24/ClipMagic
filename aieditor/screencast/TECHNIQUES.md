## The rules that matter most

1. **The edit alternates screen ↔ face; that alternation is the main motion.** Screencast 68–74 % of runtime, spans p50 10–18 s, A-roll blocks p50 5–6.5 s. 37 % of all ≥ 3 s screen holds end on the cut to A-roll. Cuts sit on sentence starts (picture leads by ~0.1 s).
2. **Screencast ↔ A-roll is a cut, not a move.** ≈ 99 % hard in the refs; spans are not zoomed out first (up to half end still zoomed). **Jake's rule 5:** use the very short bubble-first fade (TR05) instead of a plain hard cut.
3. **Within one app: hard cut. Change of world or time: 3–8 f linear dissolve with the zoom held**, then a zoom-out or pan within 0–6 f (TR01–TR03, PN05).
4. **Zoom mildly, to one region, on the words.** ×1.2–1.4 (deep ×1.5–1.65 only for one named control); D 34–48 f, cubic-bezier(.31,.10,.22,1) on log-zoom; the move starts ≈ 0.8 s before the named word and lands on it. **Jake:** end centre-middle; never earlier than the sentence that names it; straight to the target.
5. **Already zoomed and the next subject is nearby → pan, don't out-and-in.** Constant zoom, (.33,.02,.27,.96), D ≈ 24+0.035·px f; reading pans vertical, card pans horizontal, follow-to-next-click pans up to ~700 px.
6. **Holds are short.** Camera-static p50 ≤ 1.4 s, p90 5–7.4 s; 17–25 motion events per screencast minute. **Jake's rule 2:** if there is no cut there must be movement (centred slow push ZM10 is the refs' filler).
7. **Bubble is fixed; it only disappears for what is under it.** The refs fade it (6–15 f) when the framed/clicked subject is top-right and bring it back (11 f) on release. **Jake:** hide only while a click/type acts under it.
8. **Results first and big; never waiting.** Generations are skipped by a time-skip dissolve or a state cut; typing appears at once or in chunks (Jake: typed zoomed out, then fade to the result).
9. **Text only where it says something literal**: hook lines, name, "Link in the description" on the word, the tool logo when named, subscribe/socials in the outro — all lower-centre over the A-roll, never over the screencast. **Jake's rule 6:** always on the black→transparent gradient.
10. **No boxes, arrows, circles, spotlights or click ripples** in any ref; the only annotations are the marker wipe and one orange label.

## Corrections to earlier specs

1. **Screencast ↔ A-roll transitions (sc6 / SYSTEM §0 rule 5):** "88 % exit / 94 % entry hard, the rest a dissolve variant" → of the 24 non-hard detections, 21 are bubble hide/return events inside screencasts; only 2 are real boundary dissolves (ref 3 0:47.35 to A-roll; ref 5 0:35.8 bubble-first to B-roll). Refs are ≈ 99 % hard. Jake's bubble-first rule stands as his rule, not a reference statistic.
2. **Bubble visibility (kwys-screencast):** "hidden only twice" (ref 2) → 13 hide events across refs 2–5 (≈ 2/3/5/3), triggered by framing/clicking a top-right subject (incl. pure zooms toward top-right buttons). Jake's narrower rule (actions only) wins.
3. **A-roll jump cuts (kwys-annotations C2):** "no punch-ins at jump cuts" holds for ref 2 only; refs 3–5 alternate medium ↔ close-up (~1.3–1.5×) across jump cuts (CUT07).
